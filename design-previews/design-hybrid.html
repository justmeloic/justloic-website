ESSAY REFLECTION

Geometric intuition lost in algebraic translation

Much of North American undergraduate mathematics is spent reinforcing algebraic representations at the expense of geometric intuition. General Relativity is unintuitive for the same reason a probability distribution is unintuitive in first-year Statistics: you were simply not in the business of thinking about numbers this way. Intuition is not inherited, it is acquired.

— Loïc Muhirwa
