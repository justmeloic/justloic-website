Geometric Intuition Lost in Algebraic Translation

Much of undergraduate mathematics is spent reinforcing algebraic representations at the expense of geometric intuition. This is what gives the illusion that concepts like General Relativity or high-dimensional probability distributions are inherently unintuitive. Intuition is not an innate property—it is an acquired lens.

— Loïc Muhirwa

The Mind Projection Fallacy

“Common language has an almost universal tendency to disguise epistemological statements by putting them into a grammatical form which suggests an ontological statement... asserting that one's private thoughts exist externally in Nature.”
